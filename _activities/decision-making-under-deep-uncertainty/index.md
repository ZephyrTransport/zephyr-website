## Mission Statement

To advance the methods, tools, and practice of decision-making under deep uncertainty in transportation planning, enabling more robust and responsible decisions in a rapidly changing world.

## Goals

We are modelers, so this initiative takes a model-centric view of DMDU, but a big part of DMDU is moving the focus away from model forecasts and towards getting to better decisions! We seek to:

 - Complement predictive applications of models with insights generated from exploratory applications
 - Promote use of models in concert with decision makers and stakeholders
 - Facilitate an adaptive planning process where multiple futures are considered and immediate steps are taken to maintain flexibility and resiliency
