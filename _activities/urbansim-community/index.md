### Objective

The UrbanSim Community is composed of public agency planners and managers using UrbanSim.  Similar in spirit to the [ActivitySim]({{ '/activities/activitysim' | relative_url }}) consortium, the group brings together agencies to share land use modeling experience, as well as to coordinate development priorities, and to jointly support the maintenance and development of open source land use modeling software.
