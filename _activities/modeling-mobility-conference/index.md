MoMo (the Modeling Mobility Conference) is the premier forum for professionals working with travel models, transportation data, and quantitative analysis, covering topics like activity-based modeling, transit modeling, land use integration, and emerging AI applications in transportation planning.

MoMo is organized by an all-volunteer group of transportation professionals from the public sector, private consulting, vendors, and academia. Since the volunteer organizers can't independently handle contracts, venue booking, and funding, the Zephyr Foundation acts as MoMo's sponsoring 501(c)(3) organization.
