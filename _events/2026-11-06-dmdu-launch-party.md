Join us for the **DMDU Launch Party** with **Mary Milkovits and Flavia Tsang** in the **Zephyr Lounge!**

Building on years of foundational work established across prior national committee efforts, the **Zephyr Foundation** is establishing a dedicated home for the **Decision Making Under Deep Uncertainty (DMDU) activity!** Our goal is to bridge research and practice by equipping planners and agency leaders with open, actionable tools. 

Come learn what the DMDU activity is all about, play a fun round of DMDU Jeopardy!, and explore ways to get involved. Whether you’re new to DMDU or already putting it into practice, we’d love to have you join us!

Friday, November 6, at 2:15 PM Eastern / 11:15 AM Pacific in the Zephyr Lounge on Discord.

**Note:** You'll need access to the Zephyr Forum Discord server to join the Lounge.
