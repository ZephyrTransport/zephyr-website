MoMo 2027 is the 2nd Modeling Mobility Conference, continuing the legacy of the Modeling Mobility Conference as the premier forum for professionals working with travel models, transportation data, and quantitative analysis. The conference brings together public and private sector practitioners, researchers, students, and everyone involved in developing, analyzing, and applying transportation data and tools to connect, learn, share, and build lasting professional relationships.

Join us Monday, April 12th through Thursday, April 15th, 2027, in Tempe, Arizona, on the campus of Arizona State University. MoMo 2027 is co-sponsored by the Zephyr Foundation and the ASU Ira A. Fulton Schools of Engineering.

A call for abstracts for MoMo 2027 will be announced later in 2026 — visit [ModelingMobility.org](https://modelingmobility.org/) to sign up for notifications or volunteer to help organize, or email [modelingmobility@zephyrtransport.org](mailto:modelingmobility@zephyrtransport.org) with questions.
