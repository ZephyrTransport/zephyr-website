Please join us for a discussion exploring international approaches to planning for uncertainty, featuring presentations by Amy Anderson from the UK Department for Transport and Naomi Stein from EBP US.

The session will be hosted by Zephyr’s Decision Making Under Uncertainty (DMDU) co-lead Flavia Tsang of the Metropolitan Transportation Commission and Zephyr Foundation Board member Mark Bradley.

Recommended readings that provide background and additional context:

 - [**Practices in the US:** NCHRP Project 08-154: Developing a Guide for Incorporating Uncertainty into Long-Range Transportation Planning](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/29359)

 - [**Practices in the UK:** The Use of Scenarios in Transport Modelling and Appraisal – A Think Piece Drawing on Literature](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a0c3c82c75cc34a8ff8f4e6/use-of-scenarios-in-transport-modelling.pdf)
