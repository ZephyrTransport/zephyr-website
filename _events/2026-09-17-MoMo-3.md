Understanding transportation system performance requires both strong modeling techniques and real-world data insights. This session brings together research on infrastructure impacts, model variability, and big data integration to better interpret and apply travel model results in planning and policy.

Host: Marty Milkovits -- Moderator: Hailey Amundson

<h3>Speakers</h3>

**Joe Broach** (Oregon Metro) -- New Methods for Measuring Regional Mobility: Augmenting a Regional Travel Model with Big Data

**Gaurav Vyas** (Bentley) -- Rolling the Dice with ABM - A systematic study of microsimulation variability

**Greg Erhardt** (Univ Kentucky) -- Measuring the Contribution of Additional Lane Miles on the Increase in U.S. Vehicle Miles Traveled from 1980 to 2019
