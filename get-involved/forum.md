The **Zephyr Forum** is our online community where transportation and land use professionals ask questions, share ideas, collaborate on projects, and connect through discussion channels, direct messages, voice, and video.

We're launching the Forum as a pilot. If the community finds it valuable, it will become Zephyr's primary online discussion space.

## Why Discord?

Yes, it's the app gamers made famous. These days it's also where many technical and open-source communities collaborate. Think of it as Slack with better voice channels and a more active community feel.

## What You'll Find

- Ask questions and get technical advice.
- Share ideas, resources, and projects.
- Join the **Zephyr Lounge** for voice and video conversations.
- Connect with transportation professionals throughout the community.

## Getting Started

The Forum works in any web browser, but we recommend **[installing the Discord desktop or mobile app](https://discord.com/download)** so you'll receive notifications for new messages, mentions, and activity.

<div class="z-note-callout" markdown="1">

**Before you join**

- Access requires administrator approval.
- Please use your real name so members know who they're talking with.
- Zephyr may establish community guidelines and manage membership to maintain a welcoming and professional environment.

**Zephyr Lounge Tip**

Entering the Zephyr Lounge automatically connects your audio. There isn't a separate **Join Call** button, so mute your microphone—or be ready to say hello—before entering.
