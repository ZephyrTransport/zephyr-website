Zephyr is governed by a Board of Directors supported by the Founding Stakeholders and a team of Instigators.

### <i class="fa fa-institution" style="color:#{{ site.highlight-color }}" aria-hidden="true"></i> Board of Directors

An initial Board was elected by the Zephyr Founding Stakeholders in order to reflect a diversity of interests, industries, and backgrounds. In Spring of each year, roughly a third of the Board will be elected by the [Zephyr Membership](/membership).
