## Objectives

- Promote a broad understanding of practical approaches to effectively inform decision-makers faced with uncertainty underpinning baseline assumptions in travel forecasting and planning.
- Advance the state of the practice in the specification, development, and application of appropriate quantitative tools to support DMDU
- Advance DMDU in organizations
- Continue the work of the TRB Travel Forecasting DMDU initiative

## Goals
We are modelers, so this initiative takes a model-centric view of DMDU, but a big part of DMDU is moving the focus away from model forecasts and towards getting to better decisions! 

 - Complement predictive applications of models with insights generated from exploratory applications
 - Promote use of models in concert with decision makers and stakeholders
 - Facilitate an adaptive planning process where multiple futures are considered and immediate steps are taken to maintain flexibility and resiliency
