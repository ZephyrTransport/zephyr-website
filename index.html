Spotlight

TMIP: Travel Model Improvement Program — In partnership with the Federal Highway Administration (FHWA), Zephyr supports the TMIP community by moderating an active email discussion list connecting more than 1,600 travel demand forecasting professionals and maintaining a searchable archive spanning more than two decades of webinars, technical discussions, and community knowledge.

Visit the active listserv » Explore TMIP resources »
Spotlight

Network Data Standard and Management Tools — A Zephyr-led initiative advancing GMNS (General Modeling Network Specification), an open data format for sharing routable travel model networks across platforms, alongside an ecosystem of open-source tools—NetworkWrangler, OSM2GMNS, and Path4GMNS—for building and working with GMNS networks.

View the GMNS spec » Explore network standards & tools »
Spotlight

MoMo: Modeling Mobility Conference — Zephyr serves as the sponsoring organization behind MoMo, the premier conference for travel modeling, transportation data, and quantitative analysis, run by an all-volunteer group of public-sector, consulting, vendor, and academic professionals covering everything from activity-based modeling to emerging AI applications in transportation planning.

Visit ModelingMobility.org » Explore MoMo details »
